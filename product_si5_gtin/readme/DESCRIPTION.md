Look up a product by its barcode (GTIN/EAN) on [SI5 Global](https://global.si5.com.br)
and review the suggested name, internal note (brand, category), gross weight and
image before applying any of them. NCM and CEST are shown as a reference; install
`product_si5_gtin_l10n_br_fiscal` to apply them to `l10n_br_fiscal` products.
