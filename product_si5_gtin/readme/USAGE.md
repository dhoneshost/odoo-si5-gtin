1. Open a product (or the specific variant) with a barcode and click
   **Look up GTIN with SI5**.
2. Each row shows current and suggested values. Turn on **Replace** only for
   the fields you want.
3. For the image, click **Download and preview**, check it, then
   turn on **Replace** in the picture row.
4. Name, internal note, NCM and CEST belong to the product template: turn on the
   confirmation about shared data, then **Apply selected fields**.

If the product changes after the lookup, run it again. A review can be applied
once. Restricted circulation codes (prefixes 2xx, 040–049) are rejected.
