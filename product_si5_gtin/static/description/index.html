Product GTIN lookup with SI5 Global

Type the barcode, review the suggestions, apply only what you want

Fill product name, picture, gross weight and Brazilian fiscal codes (NCM / CEST) from SI5 Global, a catalogue with millions of GTINs from Brazilian retail. Nothing is written without your review.

1. One click on the product

Open a product with a barcode and click Look up GTIN with SI5.

2. Review current × suggested

Current data on the left, suggestions on the right. Every checkbox starts unchecked. The picture is downloaded only when you confirm, validated (format, size, dimensions) and shown before being applied.

3. NCM and CEST for the Brazilian localization

With the companion module product_si5_gtin_l10n_br_fiscal (installed automatically with the OCA l10n_br_fiscal), the review also offers the NCM and CEST records from the fiscal tables, only on exact matches and only CEST linked to the NCM. Fiscal classification stays the responsibility of your company and accountant.

4. Done

Features

  • Name, internal note (brand, category, source), gross weight and picture
  • WebP pictures converted for Odoo thumbnails and PDF reports
  • Single-variant products get the template picture; multi-variant keeps it on the variant
  • Extra confirmation for data shared by variants / companies
  • UPC-A (12 digits), EAN-8/13, GTIN-14; restricted prefixes rejected
  • One token per company in Settings › SI5 Global (admins only)
  • Hardened network client: fixed hosts, TLS, size limits, no secrets in errors or logs
  • English and Brazilian Portuguese

Configuration

  1. Create a free account at global.si5.com.br
  2. Create an API token in Minha conta
  3. Paste it in Settings › General Settings › SI5 Global

The free plan has a daily quota. Paid plans add more queries and the full fiscal sheet (IBPT, IPI, PIS/COFINS, ST, IBS/CBS by state): see plans.

Em português

Consulte o produto pelo código de barras no SI5 Global e preencha nome, foto, peso, NCM e CEST com revisão antes de gravar: nada vem marcado, você escolhe campo a campo. Com a localização brasileira da OCA (l10n_br_fiscal), o módulo complementar oferece o NCM e o CEST das tabelas fiscais. Crie sua conta grátis em global.si5.com.br, gere o token em Minha conta e cole em Configurações › SI5 Global.
