NCM and CEST suggestions from the barcode

Companion of Product GTIN Lookup for the OCA Brazilian fiscal engine (l10n_br_fiscal). Installed automatically when both are present.

Exact matches only

An NCM or CEST is offered only when the exact code exists in l10n_br_fiscal.ncm / l10n_br_fiscal.cest. No guessing of a "closest" classification.

CEST × NCM, said plainly

A CEST that the fiscal tables link to another NCM is not offered. When the tables link it to the target NCM, the review says so; when the CEST has no NCM link in the tables, or the product has no NCM, it is marked not verified.

You confirm

One switch each, never pre-selected, plus a confirmation because NCM and CEST live on the product template.

What it does and does not do

  • Writes only NCM and CEST on the product template, and only the ones you switch on.
  • When the NCM changes, l10n_br_fiscal itself derives the Fiscal Product Genre (NCM chapter) and the Export UoM from it; the review shows this note.
  • Does not set fiscal type, ICMS origin, tax rates, tax definitions, operations or any other fiscal configuration.
  • NCM and CEST are references from the SI5 Global catalogue: confirm them with your accountant.

Maturity: Stable

Stable for the assisted NCM/CEST review workflow, not fiscal certification. Only exact non-Ex NCM records are proposed; stale reviews and incompatible retained CEST pairs are rejected without silently clearing data. Unknown NCM/CEST relationships remain marked as not verified. Your accountant must validate classifications for your business. Production observation is not claimed.

Data sent to the external service

Nothing extra. The NCM and CEST come in the same lookup made by Product GTIN Lookup, which asks for explicit consent each time and sends only the product barcode, the company API token and a module User-Agent to SI5 Global (global.si5.com.br). No other Odoo data is sent.
